Supercar Collection

Welcome to The Car-nival! Explore our curated collection of extraordinary supercars, featuring some of the most impressive vehicles ever created.

McLaren Speedtail

The McLaren Speedtail is a hypercar renowned for its extreme performance and aerodynamic design. It boasts a hybrid powertrain combining a twin-turbo 4.0-liter V8 with an electric motor, producing a total of 1,035 horsepower.

Mercedes-AMG ONE

Featured prominently in Forza Horizon 5, the Mercedes-AMG ONE is a Formula 1-inspired hypercar with a hybrid powertrain, boasting a 1.6-liter turbocharged V6 engine and four electric motors, producing a combined output of 1,048 bhp.

Koenigsegg Jesko

A rare gem in Forza Horizon 5, the Jesko features a 5.0-liter twin-turbocharged V8 engine producing 1280 hp (1600 hp on E85), coupled with a 9-speed Koenigsegg Light Speed Transmission, making it one of the fastest cars in the world.

Lamborghini Sesto Elemento

One of the most coveted vehicles in Forza Horizon 5, the Sesto Elemento is built primarily of carbon fiber, boasting a 570 horsepower V10 engine and a curb weight of just 999 kg (2,202 lbs), offering an exceptional power-to-weight ratio.
